About Teja Shariff, CPA

Mr. Shariff has been in practice for over thirty-five years and brings a wealth of knowledge and experience. His dedication to client service and technical excellence has made Shariff & Company, LLC a trusted name throughout Connecticut.

Teja's approach combines traditional values with modern strategies, ensuring that every client receives personalized attention and forward-thinking solutions. Whether you are an individual seeking tax planning or a business owner navigating complex financial decisions, Teja provides the guidance and expertise you need.

Teja is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and a member of the Connecticut Society of Certified Public Accountants.
